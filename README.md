# Unilever Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

In August 2026, I completed a month-long Data Analytics course, marking my first in-depth learning experience in data analytics and business intelligence. As a practical outcome, I developed this beginner-level Power BI project using a Unilever sales dataset sourced from Kaggle.

During the summer break, I applied the fundamentals of data analytics through hands-on practice, including basic data cleaning, simple calculations, DAX measures, and data modeling using a Star Schema. I then transformed the prepared data into an interactive dashboard designed to answer five key business questions.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall sales performance in terms of total sales, net sales, and discounts?
2. How do sales trends change over time?
3. Which are the top 10 brands by sales volume?
4. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
5. How do sales figures vary across products, brands, categories, and distribution channels?

## Skills & Tools

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Basic data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Creating measures for sales analysis.
- **Data Modeling:** Designing a Star Schema and establishing relationships between tables.
- **Business Analysis:** Translating sales data into answers to key business questions.

## Learning Journey & Future Goals

This project represents my first step toward developing practical skills in data analytics and business intelligence. It serves as a foundation for further learning and more advanced analytical work.

My next major milestone is a Business Intelligence project planned for the first semester of 2027. Building on this experience, I aim to develop a significantly more advanced project incorporating statistical analysis, deeper business insights, and more sophisticated analytical techniques.

This project marks the beginning of my journey toward becoming a skilled data and business intelligence analyst.
