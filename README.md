## Overview

In August 2026, I completed a month-long Data Analytics course, marking my first in-depth learning experience in data analytics and business intelligence. As a practical outcome, I developed this dashboard to apply my newly acquired skills in data cleaning, DAX measures, and Star Schema data modeling, focusing on sales performance and product insights.

This project represents my first step toward developing practical skills in data analytics and business intelligence. It serves as a foundation for further learning and more advanced analytical work.

During the summer break, I undertook this project to strengthen my practical skills and prepare for my upcoming university Advanced Business Analytics project in the first semester of 2027, where I will be developing stronger business questions, more advanced data quality assessment and preprocessing, applying statistical methods for data cleaning, refining data modeling and relationships, and creating more effective dashboards that communicate actionable business insights.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall sales performance in terms of total sales, net sales, and discounts?
2. How do sales trends change over time?
3. Which are the top 10 brands by sales volume?
4. Which product categories generate the highest sales?
5. How do sales figures vary across products, brands, categories, and distribution channels?

## Skills & Tools

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query:** Basic data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Creating measures for sales analysis.
- **Data Modeling:** Designing a Star Schema and establishing relationships between tables.
- **Business Analysis:** Translating sales data into answers to key business questions.
